What makes it different from Hevy, Strong, or just using my Notes app?

Speed. You type a workout like a plain note and it becomes structured sets — then you log by holding a number and sliding, no keyboard. It's as fast as your Notes app, but it actually understands sets, reps and progress. And it's one app for lifts, food, weight, cardio and photos instead of five.

What platforms does it support?

iPhone (iOS). An Android version may come later depending on demand.

What happens to my data?

Your data syncs to your private account. No ads, and we don't sell your data. Sign in with Apple is supported.

What's with the monkey?

That's Banaño — your gym buddy. He levels up when you log and gets dramatically sad when you ghost the gym. Totally optional, but most people keep him around.
