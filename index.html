Common questions

Everything you'd want to know before joining.

How does the $19.99 founder deal work?

The first 100 people on the waitlist get a personal founder code by email at launch. Open the App Store, tap "Redeem Code", enter it, and your first year is $19.99 instead of $39.99 — a $20 saving. After year 1 it renews at the regular $39.99/yr. Cancel anytime through the App Store, no penalties. Codes are limited to the first 100 sign-ups, one per person.

When does Monolift launch?

Soon — we're in the final stretch before App Store submission. Waitlist members get the App Store link the moment it goes live, plus their founder code.

What's the difference between Monolift and other gym apps?

Three things: drag-to-dial logging (log a set in 5 seconds, no keyboard), a monkey buddy that emotionally reacts to your workouts, and a conversational AI coach that flags plateaus. Most gym apps are spreadsheets in disguise. Monolift makes opening the app something you want to do.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

You pick a plan and confirm with your Apple ID — no charge during the 7 days. If you cancel before day 7, you're not charged. If you don't cancel, your card is automatically charged on day 7 and your subscription begins. Cancel anytime via your Apple ID → Subscriptions.

What platforms?

iOS first (iPhone + iPad). Android port may come later based on demand.

My data — what happens to it?

Your data syncs to your private cloud account. No ads. No selling data. HealthKit integration is optional. Apple Sign In supported.

Why a monkey?

Because every other gym app is a spreadsheet with a fitness logo. The monkey is your buddy — reacts when you log a PR, roasts you when you ghost the gym, celebrates your streak. It's the difference between an app you use because you "should" and one you actually want to open.
