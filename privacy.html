Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Monolift ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Monolift mobile application ("the App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (if you sign up via email) — used to authenticate your account.
  • Apple ID identifier (if you sign up with Sign in with Apple) — stored as an opaque user identifier.
  • First name (optional) — collected during onboarding to personalize the experience.

Fitness & Health Data

  • Workout logs — exercise names, sets, reps, weights, notes you choose to enter.
  • Body measurements — weight, height, units of measurement.
  • Nutrition data — meals, calories, macros that you choose to log.
  • Water intake — entries you choose to log.
  • Cardio sessions — duration, distance, type that you choose to log.
  • Progress photos — photos you choose to capture or import.

App Usage Data

  • App settings — units, theme, notification preferences.
  • Pet companion state — your monkey buddy's mood and streak data.
  • AI usage counts — to enforce rate limits and abuse prevention.
  • Subscription status — purchase history (handled by Apple and RevenueCat).

Diagnostic Data

We do not currently collect crash reports or analytics. If we add diagnostic tools in the future, this policy will be updated and consent obtained where required.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the App's features.
  • Sync your data across your devices.
  • Process AI coach requests (workout suggestions, nutrition parsing, photo recognition).
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., greet you by name).
  • Send the notifications you opt in to (workout reminders, trial-end reminders).
  • Enforce subscription entitlements.
  • Prevent abuse and fraud.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third parties to operate the App:

  • Supabase — secure cloud database and authentication. Stores your account and synced fitness data. supabase.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. Receives anonymized purchase events. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • OpenAI — powers the AI coach. We send the prompt content you provide; OpenAI does not train on your data per its API policy. openai.com/policies
  • Apple — App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple, push notifications. Subject to Apple's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers. Ever.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored locally on your device first, and synced to Supabase if you are signed in. All network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Supabase uses row-level security so that one account cannot access another account's data.

5. Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data — visible inside the App at all times.
  • Export your data — contact privacy@monolift.app for a copy.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Account → Delete Account, or email privacy@monolift.app. Deletion removes your data within 30 days.
  • Correct inaccurate data — edit it directly in the App, or contact us.
  • Object to processing — stop using the App and delete your account.

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA), you have additional rights to access, correct, delete, and port your data. Contact privacy@monolift.app to exercise them.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. After account deletion, data is purged from production within 30 days and from backups within 90 days. Anonymized usage counts may be retained longer for abuse prevention.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a child has signed up, contact privacy@monolift.app and we will remove the account.

8. International Transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. By using the App, you consent to such transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the App or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.

10. Contact

Questions, concerns, or requests:

Email: privacy@monolift.app
