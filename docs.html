Logging workouts

Notes, not forms

A workout in Monolift is a plain-text note. The first line is the title, and each exercise is one line:

Push Day
Bench Press: 135 lbs 8, 8, 6
Overhead Press: 95 lbs 10, 10
> felt strong today
  • Exercise line format: Name: weight unit reps, reps, reps. The : trigger and , rep separator are configurable in Settings → Input Format.
  • Units: lbs, kg, or plt (plates).
  • Comments: any line starting with > is a free-text cue or note. Bare lines like "Warm-Up" work as section labels.

Log a set in seconds — no keyboard

Tap a weight or rep number and drag to dial it in. Long-press for more options. Everything on an exercise line is tappable, so updating today's numbers never needs typing.

Import a workout from anywhere

Paste free-form text or snap a photo of a written program — the AI converts it into Monolift format for you.

Rest timers

Two kinds of timer

  • >notetimer:90 on its own line at the top of a note — one global rest timer (in seconds) for the whole workout.
  • >timer:135 on its own line right after an exercise — a timer scoped to that exercise only.

Timer position and vibration are configurable in Settings.

PRs & progress

Automatic personal records

Every set you log feeds your exercise history. Monolift tracks your peak weight per exercise and unit automatically — no separate PR entry, ever. The Progress tab charts your lifts over week, month, year, or all time.

AI coach

A coach that knows your numbers

The AI coach (a Pro feature) reads your actual training history — real weights, real dates. Ask it to analyze a lift, plan a session, or explain a plateau, and it answers from your data, not generic advice.

What it can do for you

  • Create and edit workouts — it drafts the note, you approve it with one tap before anything is saved.
  • Log food and body weight — tell it what you ate; it estimates macros and proposes the entry.
  • Read your logs on request — nutrition, body weight, water, and training history.
  • Change app settings — say "switch me to dark mode and set my protein goal to 180" and it proposes the change; you approve before it applies.
  • Pick canonical exercises — it matches exercises to the built-in library so you get demonstration guides.

Every change the coach proposes shows an approval card first. Nothing is written without your OK (unless you enable auto-approve in Settings → AI).

Staying in its lane

The coach only talks training, nutrition, recovery, and Monolift itself. It declines everything else — it's a lifting coach, not a general chatbot.

Nutrition

Three ways to log food

  • Type it — "2 eggs and toast with butter" gets parsed into calories and macros.
  • Photograph it — point the camera at your plate; the AI identifies the food and estimates portions.
  • Ask the coach — mention what you ate in chat and it proposes the entry.

Daily calorie, protein, carb, and fat goals live in Settings → Nutrition, with your day tracked against them on the Log tab. Micronutrients are tracked too.

Water

Hydration tracking

Log water in fl oz, ml, or cups with a slider or quick chips — your pick. Set a daily goal and choose your glass style. It sits on the Log tab alongside meals.

Body weight & progress photos

Weigh-ins and trends

Log body weight from the Log tab (or ask the coach). Set a target weight and watch the trend chart. Progress photos attach to your timeline and can be locked behind Face ID in Settings.

The monkey

Your training partner

The monkey mascot reflects your consistency — train and it thrives, slack and it sulks. It evolves through ranks as you put in work, celebrates PRs, and sends the occasional nudge.

Notification categories (reminders, celebrations, greetings, re-engagement), quiet hours, and a daily cap are all in Settings → Notifications.

Squad

Lift with your people

Invite friends to a squad: see everyone's monkeys, a shared activity feed, and kudos on each other's sessions. You control the numbers — Settings → Squad decides whether squadmates see your actual weights and reps or just that you trained.

Settings worth knowing

Make it yours

  • Input Format — trigger character, rep separator, visible units, exercise/weight/rep colors.
  • Appearance — light/dark, accent color, font, text size.
  • AI — auto-approve coach actions, proactive suggestions.
  • Notifications — categories, quiet hours, max per day.
  • Log layout — reorder or hide the macro, chart, meal, water, and weight blocks.

Or just ask the coach to change most of these for you.

Your data

Local-first, synced when signed in

Everything is stored on your device first, so the app works fully offline. Signing in adds cloud sync across devices. We never sell your data — see the Privacy Policy.
