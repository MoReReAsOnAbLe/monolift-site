Account & Subscription

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Monolift → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Pro features until the period ends.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Monolift → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com within 90 days of purchase. We cannot issue refunds directly.

How do I delete my account?

Open Monolift → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your data is permanently deleted within 30 days. Or email privacy@monolift.app.

How do I export my data?

Email privacy@monolift.app from the account address. We'll send a JSON archive within 30 days.

Using the App

The AI coach says I need Pro. Why?

The AI features (coach chat, food-photo parsing, AI workout suggestions) require an active Monolift Pro subscription. Start a free 7-day trial from Settings → Subscription.

My data is missing after signing in on a new phone.

Pull-to-refresh on the home tab. If still missing, sign out and back in — this re-syncs from cloud. If still missing, email support@monolift.app.

Sync seems stuck.

Monolift is local-first — your data is safe on device even if sync is offline. Check connection, then pull-to-refresh. Sync will catch up automatically.

I'm getting too many monkey notifications.

Settings → Notifications → adjust frequency or turn off categories you don't want.

The monkey looks sad — what does that mean?

Your monkey reflects your streak. Skip workouts and it gets sad. Log a workout and it cheers up. It's a gentle nudge, not a judgment.

Privacy & Security

Does Monolift sell my data?

No. We do not sell data to advertisers, brokers, or any third party. See our Privacy Policy.

Does the AI train on my chats?

No. We use the OpenAI API, which does not train on data sent via API per OpenAI's policy.
